# Pledge (For “RIKYU” Users)

<div class="rules" markdown>

To Mr. Satoshi Matsuoka, Director<br>
RIKEN Center for Computational Science

In obtaining an account for “RIKYU” (AI for Science Supercomputer) and using “RIKYU”, I hereby pledge to comply with the following terms and conditions.

## 1.　Basic Policy { #article-1 }

<div class="rules-items" markdown>

(1) The user shall use “RIKYU” appropriately for the purpose of contributing to the advancement of science and technology, and ensures that such use is appropriate in light of applicable laws and regulations and accepted social norms.

(2) The User shall comply with applicable laws and regulations, as well as the Regulations for Use of “RIKYU” and regulations stipulated by RIKEN and RIKEN Center for Computational Science (hereinafter referred to as "R-CCS").

(3) The user shall be responsible for the use of “RIKYU”.

</div>

## 2.　Purpose Restrictions { #article-2 }

<div class="rules-items" markdown>

(1) Users shall not use this system for illegal, improper, or harmful purposes.

(2) Even if the research project in which the user participates at the time of use has not yet been determined, the user shall comply with laws and regulations related to security export control and shall not use this system for the development or provision of technologies regulated under such laws and regulations, or for any other corresponding purposes.

</div>

## 3.　Usage Environment { #article-3 }

<div class="rules-items" markdown>

(1) The User shall appropriately manage the account and authentication information, shall endeavor to prevent unauthorized use, and be responsible for all activities that occur under their account and authentication information.

(2) Users must not lend their account to third parties for use.

</div>

## 4.　Provision of Information Regarding Usage { #article-4 }

<div class="rules-items" markdown>

(1) The Center may, when necessary for the proper operation of this System, request users to provide information regarding their usage status.

(2) Users shall comply with such a request when made pursuant to the preceding paragraph.

</div>

## 5.　Breach { #article-5 }

If the user violates this agreement or related regulations, or if false declarations are discovered, R-CCS may suspend account usage and take other necessary measures.

## 6.　Liability { #article-6 }

If the user causes damage to R-CCS or any third party due to reasons attributable to the user, the user shall be responsible for compensating for such damages.

## 7.　Disclaimer by R-CCS { #article-7 }

<div class="rules-items" markdown>

(1) RIKEN and R-CCS shall not be held responsible for any damages incurred by users in connection with the use of “RIKYU”.

</div>

## 8.　Other { #article-8 }

Matters not specified in this pledge shall be governed by regulations separately established by R-CCS.
